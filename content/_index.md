      text: |-
        My work centres on **spiroarteriocardiorhythmography (SACR)** — a method that records
        heart rate, beat-to-beat arterial pressure and respiratory flow at the same time, and
        so allows cardiac, vascular and respiratory regulation to be assessed as one coupled
        system rather than as separate signals.

        Current lines of research:

        - cardiorespiratory interaction in health and disease, and its reorganisation under load
        - autonomic markers of functional and non-functional overreaching in elite athletes
        - cardiovascular response to manual therapy and to physical rehabilitation programmes in arterial hypertension and after percutaneous coronary intervention
        - rehabilitation of patients after blast trauma and spinal cord injury
        - sex differences in cardiorespiratory regulation and in the response to paced breathing

        Collaboration enquiries are welcome.
